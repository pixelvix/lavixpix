About Me

Photography has been a passion of mine for the past 15 years. I've developed an affinity for low-light and macro photography. I'm constantly taking photos on my adventures and candids of friends and family, trying to capture the vast range of emotions in this world.

As a trans* person, I strive to capture the heart of the subject through a lens that subverts the dominate social gaze of gender. I love to capture the parts of people that not everyone gets to see, but is who they truly are at heart, regardless of gender.
